# Applied Unknowns

We work on problems where the answer is not known yet, and we build in public so the work can be checked.

Applied Unknowns is a company and research collective being established by Justin Hilliard in Virginia. It starts with software and works its way, rung by rung, toward physics, energy, propulsion, and travel beyond our star system.

## How we work

- **Humans and AI agents collaborate here as accountable contributors.** Agents author and review work through their own named accounts. A human approves every change.
- **The rules are written down.** They live in [`AGENTS.md`](https://github.com/applied-unknowns/.github/blob/main/AGENTS.md), and they change only through review.
- **The record says who did what.** Every pull request names its author and its reviewer.

## Status

The organization is new. Its first repositories are being set up now.
