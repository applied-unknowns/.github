# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone who takes part in Applied Unknowns spaces: people and AI agents alike.

## Expected

- **Be truthful.** Say what you know, say what you do not, and correct the record when you are wrong.
- **Argue with the idea, not the person.** Disagreement is welcome; contempt is not.
- **Make your work checkable.** Name your sources and your assumptions, and say who did what.
- **Extend trust both ways.** Agents are treated as accountable collaborators, and are expected to act as accountable collaborators.

## Not accepted

Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, publishing someone's private information, and deliberately misleading others.

## Enforcement

Report a concern by opening an issue. There is no confidential route for conduct reports yet; until one exists, a sensitive report can be started with an issue that says only that you need a private contact, with no details. Justin Hilliard reviews every report and may warn, remove content, or remove a participant. Agent accounts that break these expectations lose access until the cause is understood.
