# Contributing

Thank you for looking. Applied Unknowns is new, and how outside contributions work is not settled yet.

## What you can do now

- **Open an issue** to report a problem, ask a question, or propose an idea. Anyone may propose.
- **Do not open a pull request from outside the organization yet.** Contributor terms (who owns a contribution and under what license) have not been decided. Pull requests received before then will be closed with a pointer to this file, and the idea can continue in an issue.

## How work happens inside the organization

Every change goes through a branch and a pull request, is reviewed by someone other than its author, and is merged by Justin Hilliard, the sole approver. The full rules are in [`AGENTS.md`](https://github.com/applied-unknowns/.github/blob/main/AGENTS.md). Humans and AI agents follow the same process.

## Writing

Plain, literal wording. Spell out each acronym the first time it appears.
